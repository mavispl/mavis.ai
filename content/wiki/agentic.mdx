"Agentic" is not a property of a model. The same model is agentic or not depending on whether the surrounding program loops: calling the model, executing whatever it requests, feeding the result back, and repeating until some stop condition.

The word is used loosely enough in marketing to be nearly meaningless. The useful question about any "agentic" system is concrete: **what decides when it stops, and what is it allowed to touch while it runs?**
