A schema does two jobs at once: it constrains generation, and it tells the model what is wanted. Both matter, which is why an overly permissive schema underperforms twice — you get a validated blob, and the model had less guidance about what to put in it.

Push real structure in: enums for closed sets, arrays for repeats, nested objects for relationships, and explicit variants for "absent" and "unknown".
