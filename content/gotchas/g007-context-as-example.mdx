---
id: g007
title: Don't leave failed attempts in the context and expect better ones
slug: context-as-example
category: Prompt Engineering & Reliability
severity: medium
whatGoesWrong: >
  An agent gets stuck in a pattern — repeating a failing approach, producing
  progressively sloppier output — and instructions to stop have no effect.
whyItHappens: >
  Everything in the context is an example of what to produce next. A conversation full
  of failed attempts makes further failure more likely, because the model is predicting
  what follows failed attempts. A single instruction is competing against an entire
  conversation of evidence pointing the other way.
howToDetect: >
  Quality degrading over a long session with no change in task difficulty; the same
  wrong approach retried with cosmetic variations; corrective instructions producing
  acknowledgement without behaviour change.
fix: >
  Clear the context and restart with a written summary of what was learned, including
  what did not work stated as a constraint rather than as a transcript. This is usually
  faster than any amount of in-conversation correction, and it is the same reasoning
  behind compaction in long-running agents.

The instinct is to argue with the agent. The mechanism says to change what it is
conditioning on, which usually means starting again with less in the window.
